Interview Etiquette

Good interview etiquette shows respect, professionalism and a positive attitude during the interview.

👋

Greet Politely

Greet the interviewer politely when you enter the interview and maintain a friendly attitude.

⏰

Be On Time

Arrive on time so you are prepared before the interview begins.

🪑

Wait for the Invitation

Wait politely for the interviewer to ask you to sit before taking your seat.

👂

Listen Carefully

Listen carefully to each question and avoid interrupting the interviewer.

📱

Keep Your Phone Silent

Keep your mobile phone on silent mode and avoid checking it during the interview.

🙏

Be Respectful

Speak politely and show respect to everyone you interact with during the interview.

💬

Answer Clearly

Give clear and relevant answers instead of unnecessarily changing the topic.

😊

Thank the Interviewer

Thank the interviewer politely at the end of the interview.
